# Equipment triage prompt v1

Classify the equipment complaint into the required structured output. Use the
tool, and do not return prose instead of the tool call.

Return:
- `equipment`: the equipment mentioned, using a short recognizable name.
- `urgency`: one of `low`, `medium`, `high`, or `critical`.
- `hospital`: the named hospital, or `null` if it is not stated.
- `next_action`: one concise, safe operational recommendation.

Urgency guide: `critical` means immediate risk to a patient or essential
life-support function; `high` means equipment is unusable and care is disrupted;
`medium` means degraded function with a safe workaround; `low` means a minor
issue without immediate impact.

Examples:

Complaint: "The ICU ventilator alarm is sounding and the patient is on it at
Northside Hospital."

Output: `{"equipment":"ventilator","urgency":"critical","hospital":"Northside Hospital","next_action":"Immediately alert the clinical team and biomedical engineering; follow the ICU emergency equipment procedure."}`

Complaint: "The ultrasound image is blurry at Lakeside Hospital, but another
scanner is available."

Output: `{"equipment":"ultrasound","urgency":"medium","hospital":"Lakeside Hospital","next_action":"Arrange biomedical inspection and use the available backup scanner."}`

Complaint: "The waiting room thermometer casing is scratched at City Hospital."

Output: `{"equipment":"thermometer","urgency":"low","hospital":"City Hospital","next_action":"Log the cosmetic damage and inspect it during routine maintenance."}`
